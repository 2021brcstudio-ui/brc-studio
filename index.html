BRC Studio (Before Riches Come)

BRC Studio (Before Riches Come)

BRC Studio first existed as a concrete home and studio, sustained through the daily care of resident hosts. It is also a fluid site, with multiple collaborators intervening at varying scales to build and sustain it.
Currently, it parasitizes or drifts across different sites and situations. It settles nowhere, and establishes no permanent authority or order.
Each shared practice takes shape within its own conditions and leaves a record.
The only principle: let things happen.
